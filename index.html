Welcome to Howard Farm

Howard Farm Holiday Cottages is a collection of comfortable 4-star self-catering bungalows nestled in peaceful surroundings just outside Bude. Guests particularly praise the mature garden, the hosts and the convenient location.

Each cottage has a fully equipped kitchen, private bathroom, lounge with flat-screen TV, and outdoor seating. Whether you’re here for the beaches, coastal walks, family time or simply to unwind, you’ll find a warm welcome and a true home from home.

Explore North Cornwall

A quiet rural setting with easy access to beaches, coastal paths and classic Cornish attractions.

Howard Farm sits on Howard Lane, a short drive from the centre of Bude and the sandy beaches that make this stretch of coast so popular. You’re also well placed for day trips to Tintagel, Launceston and the wider North Cornwall coast.

  • ~2 km Bude centre — shops, restaurants, Bude Sea Pool & canal
  • ~2 km Sandymouth Beach — beautiful National Trust beach and walks
  • ~3.5 km Bude Castle & heritage centre
  • ~28 km Launceston Castle
  • ~31 km Tintagel Castle — legendary King Arthur site
  • ~62 km Newquay Cornwall Airport
